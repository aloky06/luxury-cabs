Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our luxury chauffeur and booking policies.

How far in advance should I book my luxury cab?

▾
For airport transfers and executive sedans, we recommend booking at least 2 to 4 hours in advance. For ultra-luxury cars like Rolls-Royce and Bentley or wedding convoys, booking 24–48 hours in advance ensures guaranteed vehicle availability and custom embellishments.

What is included in the fare quote?

▾
Our quotes are fully all-inclusive: vehicle hire, uniformed professional chauffeur, fuel, road tolls, state taxes, parking charges, high-speed 5G Wi-Fi, and bottled mineral water. Zero hidden surcharges.

How does airport flight tracking work?

▾
When you provide your flight number during booking, our dispatch team tracks your flight status live. If your flight is delayed or lands early, your chauffeur automatically adjusts pickup time with 60 minutes of complimentary wait time after landing.

Can I make custom stops or hire for multiple days?

▾
Yes! You can choose our "Hourly Charter" option or request bespoke multi-day outstation itineraries. Our concierge team can arrange customized travel schedules with dedicated chauffeurs.
